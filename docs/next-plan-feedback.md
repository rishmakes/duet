# Next plan: quick feedback (issue 122)

Said by the creator on 19 Oct 2026. Build this in the session after the Motion panel work. Plan in chat first.

## Why

In the early days feedback matters more than anything. When something behaves oddly, the person must be able to tell us in seconds, even with the mouse busy (dragging a window, mid-drag on the canvas).

## What the person gets

- A Feedback place at the top of the window, always there.
- A dedicated keyboard shortcut that clashes with nothing on Mac, Windows or in Duet. It works whatever they are doing, even mid-drag.
- Pressing either one takes a picture of the Duet window only (not the whole screen) at that moment, before anything else changes.
- A feedback form opens with that picture already attached. They write anything and press Send.
- They can attach more pictures.
- After sending, one thing shows, and it is prominent: "Still stuck? Book time with the creator." It opens the calendar (https://cal.com/rishmakes/coffee). Instagram as a second way, if wanted.

## Open, decide with the creator

- Where feedback lands. Not everything should become a GitHub issue. Options: Google Form, Google Sheet, a GitHub issue, something else. Pictures need a home too.
- The exact shortcut. Must be checked against macOS, Windows and Duet's own shortcuts.
- The name on the after-send prompt (creator, founder, or his name).
- His Instagram handle, if Instagram is offered.
- The exact place at the top (placement is designed in a mockup first).
